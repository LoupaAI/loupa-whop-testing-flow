Privacy policy

The customer's delivery link has no expiration date and remains accessible indefinitely, under four conditions:

1. Website exists: The website has not been deleted.

2. Product exists: The product has not been deleted from Manage Products (switching a product from Live to Draft still preserves buyer access).

3. Files exist: The files have not been deleted from the product in Loupa.

4. Order is paid: The order remains in `paid` status (refunds or payment revocations end access).

Each time the customer opens the delivery page, fresh download links are generated with a 1-hour validity window. If they expire while the page is open, reloading the page immediately generates new links.
